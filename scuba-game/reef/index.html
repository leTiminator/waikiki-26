What this is. The first thing the art bible asks for (§14.1): a depth colour-grade over a single reef, proving the atmosphere thesis — and the first consumer of TideForge output. The seabed is autotiled from the exported maskToTile map, flora is planted on its base anchors, fish play their swim clips, and bubbles/godrays/silt are exported VFX sheets. Sprites ship ungraded; the depth grade runs at runtime from depth-grade.js, the same math TideForge previews with. Diver art is a placeholder — that's the M2 hero asset.
